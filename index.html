Who We Are

Dubai’s Trusted Name in BMU Cradle

BIG WAVE TECHNICAL MAINTENANCE is a distinguished ISO Certified company based in Dubai, renowned for its expertise in BMU’s Cradle. With a solid track record spanning many years, we have contributed significantly to preserving the aesthetic appeal of the UAE through our comprehensive maintenance services.

Our team comprises highly skilled engineers and technical professionals with over 15 years of collective experience gathered from leading BMU’s cradle worldwide. We are specialists in all brands and models of BMU’s cradles — including Davit System, Monorail and Gantry

What We Do

Our Core Services

End-to-end BMU Cradle solutions — from new supply and installation through to maintenance, repair, rental, shifting and certification.

Supply & Installation

Comprehensive BMU Cradle supply and installation — delivered with precision, safety and efficiency at every step.

View Details →

Annual Maintenance (AMC)

Comprehensive, semi-comprehensive and non-comprehensive AMC plans with monthly, bi-monthly, quarterly or twice-yearly PPM services, plus 24/7 call-back support for emergencies.

Modernization

Upgrade aging BMU Cradle systems with modern components and technology to improve performance, safety and reliability without full replacement.

Repair Works

Swift diagnosis and one-time repair of mechanical and technical issues, with 24/7 emergency response from certified technicians to minimize downtime.

BMU Cradle Rent

A modern rental fleet of BMU Cradle systems with advanced safety features, tailored to each project’s specific access and maintenance requirements.

BMU Cradle Shifting

Expert dismantling, relocation and reinstallation with thorough site assessment, project management and strict adherence to safety protocols throughout.

3rd Party Certification

Full assistance obtaining third-party certification (TPI) for BMUs , guiding clients through compliance with EIAC, ENAS and Dubai standards.
